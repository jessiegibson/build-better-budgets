## Forms Required for Amendment

1. Form 1040-X, Amended U.S. Individual Income Tax Return: This is the primary form used to amend your original 1040.
Schedule E, Supplemental Income and Loss (From rental real estate, royalties, partnerships, S corporations, estates, and trusts): We'll need to revise your Schedule E to reflect the changes due to REPS.
2. Form 461, Limitation on Business Losses: This form is needed to determine the amount of business losses you can deduct.
3. Form 8582, Passive Activity Loss Limitations: This form is used to calculate the amount of passive activity losses you can deduct. Since you may qualify for REPS, this form may show that you can deduct more of your losses.
4. Detailed Records: You must maintain thorough records to substantiate your hours and expenses. This includes:
5. A log of your hours spent on each activity (e.g., property search, remodeling, marketing, tenant management, maintenance).
6. Receipts and invoices for all expenses related to the rental property.
7. Documentation of advertising and marketing efforts.
8. Records of tenant communications and maintenance requests
